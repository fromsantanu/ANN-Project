## **Chapter 18: Understanding Output**

---

### **1. What Does the Model Output Mean?**

After prediction, the model gives a number like:

* 0.2
* 0.6
* 0.85

👉 But what do these numbers actually mean?

---

### **2. Output = Probability**

Because we used **sigmoid activation**, the output is:

👉 **A probability between 0 and 1**

So:

* 0 → 0% chance
* 1 → 100% chance

---

### **3. Interpreting the Values**

Let’s understand this clearly:

| Output | Meaning        |
| ------ | -------------- |
| 0.1    | Very low risk  |
| 0.3    | Low risk       |
| 0.5    | Uncertain      |
| 0.7    | High risk      |
| 0.9    | Very high risk |

👉 Higher value = higher chance of risk

---

### **4. Converting Probability to Decision**

Usually, we need a final decision:

* Risk or No Risk

We use a **threshold**:

👉 Common choice = **0.5**

* ≥ 0.5 → Risk (1)
* < 0.5 → No Risk (0)

---

### **5. Example**

#### Case 1:

* Output = 0.8

👉 80% chance → **High Risk**

---

#### Case 2:

* Output = 0.3

👉 30% chance → **Low Risk**

---

### **6. Important Insight**

👉 The model is **not 100% certain**

It is saying:

* “I am this much confident”

---

### **7. Real-Life Analogy**

Think of weather prediction:

* 80% chance of rain → likely rain
* 20% chance → unlikely

👉 Same idea: **confidence level**

---

### **8. Why Probability Is Better**

Instead of just saying:

* Yes or No

We get:

* Degree of confidence

👉 This is more informative and useful

---

### **9. Caution**

* 0.51 and 0.99 both become “1” after threshold
* But confidence is very different

👉 Always consider probability, not just final label

---

### **🎯 Key Idea of This Chapter**

👉 **Model output = probability (confidence of prediction)**
