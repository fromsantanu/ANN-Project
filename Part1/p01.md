## **Chapter 1: What Problem Are We Solving?**

---

### **1. The Idea in Simple Words**

Imagine a doctor who has some basic information about a patient:

* Age
* Blood Pressure
* Sugar Level
* Weight
* Cholesterol

Now the doctor wants to answer one important question:

👉 *“Is this patient at risk of a disease or not?”*

This is exactly the problem we are going to solve using an **Artificial Neural Network (ANN)**.

---

### **2. Real-Life Analogy**

Think of this like a teacher predicting exam results:

* If a student studies regularly → likely to pass
* If attendance is low → risk of failing

Similarly, our model will learn patterns like:

* High BP + High sugar → higher risk
* Normal values → lower risk

So basically, we are building a **smart decision system**.

---

### **3. What Are the Inputs?**

These are the pieces of information we give to the model:

| Feature     | Meaning            |
| ----------- | ------------------ |
| Age         | Age of the patient |
| BP          | Blood Pressure     |
| Sugar       | Blood sugar level  |
| Weight      | Body weight        |
| Cholesterol | Cholesterol level  |

👉 These are called **features** in machine learning.

---

### **4. What Is the Output?**

The model will give **one output**:

* `0` → No risk
* `1` → High risk

Or sometimes:

* A probability (e.g., 0.8 = 80% risk)

👉 This is called the **prediction**.

---

### **5. Sample Data (What the Model Sees)**

Here is a small example of how our data might look:

| Age | BP  | Sugar | Weight | Cholesterol | Risk |
| --- | --- | ----- | ------ | ----------- | ---- |
| 45  | 130 | 85    | 70     | 200         | 0    |
| 60  | 150 | 120   | 80     | 240         | 1    |
| 50  | 140 | 95    | 75     | 210         | 0    |
| 65  | 160 | 130   | 85     | 260         | 1    |

👉 The last column (**Risk**) is what we want the model to learn.

---

### **6. What Are We Building?**

We are building a system that:

1. Takes patient data as input
2. Learns patterns from past data
3. Predicts whether a new patient is at risk

---

### **7. Why Is This Important?**

* Helps doctors make faster decisions
* Can be used in hospitals or health apps
* Useful in preventive healthcare
