## **Project 1: Pass / Fail Prediction**

---

### **Problem Statement**

Predict whether a student will:

* Pass (1)
* Fail (0)

---

### **Inputs (Features)**

You can use:

* Study hours
* Attendance (%)
* Previous marks
* Assignment completion (0/1)

---

### **Sample Dataset**

| Study Hours | Attendance | Marks | Assignment | Result |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----- | ---------- | ------ |
| 2           | 60         | 40    | 0          | 0      |
| 5           | 80         | 65    | 1          | 1      |
| 3           | 70         | 50    | 1          | 0      |
| 6           | 90         | 75    | 1          | 1      |

---
